# Learning & Development Analytics – Power BI

A Power BI business intelligence project analyzing employee training 
effectiveness, performance improvement, training investment, engagement, 
completion, and ROI.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Organizations invest significantly in employee training, but participation 
alone does not show whether training is delivering measurable results.

This project analyzes training data to understand:

- How training affects employee performance
- How training programs and delivery modes differ in outcomes
- How training investment relates to ROI
- How training hours and engagement vary across departments

## 📊 Dashboard

### Page 1 – Overview

Provides a high-level view of training performance using:

- Employees Trained
- Completion Rate
- Training Investment
- Average Performance Gain
- Average ROI
- Department-wise performance
- Monthly investment and performance trends
- Program-level performance and ROI

### Page 2 – Methods & Strategies

Analyzes training approaches using:

- Performance Gain by Quarter & Delivery Mode
- ROI by Program & Delivery Mode
- Performance Lift by Department
- Employee Engagement & Training Hours by Department

### Page 3 – Conclusions

Converts analysis into business actions through:

- Key Insights
- Recommendations
- Root Cause Analysis (RCA)
- SWOT Analysis

## 💡 Key Strategies

- Evaluate training using performance, engagement, completion, and ROI.
- Compare delivery modes and training programs based on outcomes.
- Identify departmental differences and training requirements.
- Monitor training performance over time.
- Align training investment with measurable outcomes.

## 📌 Recommendations

- Monitor training effectiveness using multiple KPIs.
- Review programs based on performance improvement and ROI.
- Investigate differences across departments and delivery modes.
- Use training hours and engagement to understand outcome variations.
- Regularly review training investment against business results.

## 📈 Key Metrics

| Metric | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Employees Trained | Measures training participation |
| Completion Rate | Measures training completion |
| Training Investment | Measures resources invested in training |
| Average Performance Gain | Measures improvement after training |
| Average ROI | Measures return from training |
| Training Hours | Measures employee training exposure |
| Employee Engagement | Provides context on employee participation |
| Performance Lift | Measures improvement associated with training |
